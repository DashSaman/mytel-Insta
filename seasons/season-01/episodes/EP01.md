Preserve supplied identities and colors. No on-screen subtitles or text generated by Veo. Add text and voice in editing. Maintain consistent screen direction and eyelines.

## Six production shots — generate separately
### Shot 01 (00–08s) — hook
**References:** mr-zangi.png, simo.png; living room plate.
**Action:** Mr Zangi rings furiously on the wooden side table; Simo recoils in surprise, spring body bouncing once. No humans in frame.
**Prompt suffix:** `Medium close-up on the mustard-yellow anthropomorphic rotary telephone ringing urgently on a wooden side table. The gray coiled-cord companion with red shoes flinches and bounces once. Camera gently pushes in. Keep both character designs identical to reference. No speech, no extra objects.`
**Post audio:** SFX old telephone ring; Zangi (objects-only voice): «الو! باز عاشق‌ها افتادن به جون خط!»

### Shot 02 (08–16s) — caller
**References:** amir.png; simple Amir room plate.
**Action:** Amir nervously dials the landline, glances toward door, then straightens his shirt.
**Prompt suffix:** `Medium shot of Amir, identical to supplied reference, standing beside a vintage landline telephone in a modest 1990s Iranian room at night. He nervously finishes dialing, checks the closed door, straightens his shirt and waits. One actor only, locked camera, no speaking animation.`
**Post audio:** Amir: «ملیحه؟ خودتی؟»

### Shot 03 (16–24s) — wrong answer
**References:** haj-nosratollah.png; living room plate.
**Action:** Nosratollah answers receiver, suspicious frown.
**Prompt suffix:** `Medium close-up of Haj Nosratollah, matching reference image, picking up a ringing vintage telephone receiver in a modest Iranian living room at night. His eyebrows narrow suspiciously and he holds still to listen. One actor only, slow push-in, no lip-sync.`
**Post audio:** Nosratollah: «بله؟ کیه این وقت شب؟»

### Shot 04 (24–32s) — improvised lie
**References:** amir.png; Amir room plate.
**Action:** Amir freezes, looks up as if thinking, then puts on a confident smile.
**Prompt suffix:** `Close-up of Amir holding a vintage telephone receiver. He freezes in panic, looks upward searching for an excuse, then forces an overconfident smile. One actor, stable camera, no mouth articulation, preserve facial features.`
**Post audio:** Amir: «سلام، تعمیرکار کولرم! برای سرویس زنگ زدم.»

### Shot 05 (32–40s) — punchline
**References:** haj-nosratollah.png; living room plate.
**Action:** Nosratollah squints, then looks around at the room in disbelief.
**Prompt suffix:** `Close-up of Haj Nosratollah holding the receiver. He squints in suspicion, then slowly looks around his living room with incredulity. Restrained comedic timing, single continuous shot, one actor, no lip-sync.`
**Post audio:** Nosratollah: «ما که کولر نداریم!» / Amir (through telephone, offscreen): «پس مشکل از همینه!»

### Shot 06 (40–48s) — cliffhanger
**References:** malihe.png, haj-nosratollah.png; living room plate.
**Action:** Maliheh enters frame behind Nosratollah, concerned; he turns toward her.
**Prompt suffix:** `Medium shot in the same living room. Haj Nosratollah holds a vintage phone receiver. Maliheh, identical to supplied reference, steps into frame behind him and gently gestures to interrupt. He turns his head toward her in surprise. Exactly two humans, no speaking animation, no cuts.`
**Post audio:** Maliheh: «بابا... اون تعمیرکار نیست!» / short musical sting, cut to black.
